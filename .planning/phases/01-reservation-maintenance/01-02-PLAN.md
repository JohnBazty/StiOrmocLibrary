Make the saved settings drive behaviour: Mark ready uses the pickup window, the expiry job expires waiting reservations past the limit with their own notice, and students see "Expires on" for waiting reservations.

Purpose: SPEC R1, R2 and R8 (RES-MAINT-01) and SPEC R4, R5 and R6 (RES-MAINT-02). ROADMAP success criteria 1-3: the next Mark ready uses the window; waiting reservations past the limit expire and the student is told; with the limit blank none ever expire.

</objective>
