## How

Reuse the notification recipient ledger and durable runner. Explicit device opt-in, standards-based VAPID with empty push payloads, generic lock-screen notices, an authenticated inbox on tap, deduplication, bounded retries and subscription retirement. No new dependency or scheduler. Only known HTTPS browser push services are allowed. A successful provider response means accepted, not delivered or read. Device acceptance remains separate.

Extend existing collection review with clearly labelled age-review and missing-information signals; approved subject requirements, edition, use and librarian recommendations remain authoritative. Never automatically archive holdings.

Migration 058 adds private device/outbox and document-manifest/event tables plus retention metadata. Completed jobs become eligible ten days after completion; holds block deletion. Lock the request during cleanup and hold changes. Delete only scoped document artifacts, retry failures, keep all charges, receipts, payment proofs and history. Legacy completed documents use their recorded completion time. A cleanup failure does not mark a document expired.

Place priorities and operational work before analytics. Use semantic, horizontally scrollable request tables, stable catalog actions and a Student/Faculty scoped teal/navy shopping interface. Preserve existing filters, drafts, backend rules and floating cart.
